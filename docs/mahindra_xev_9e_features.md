# Mahindra XEV 9e: Feature Breakdown
*Based on the "More Gadget Than a CAR!" review by Venom's Tech*

The Mahindra XEV 9e is positioned as a high-tech "gadget on wheels." Below is a detailed list of the features mentioned in the video.

---

### 1. Interior & Display (The "Cinemascope" Setup)
*   **Triple 12.3-inch Screens:** A massive 43-inch (110 cm) single-piece curved glass dashboard containing three separate high-definition displays:
    *   **Driver’s Cluster:** Shows navigation, driving stats, and ADAS visuals.
    *   **Central Infotainment:** Controls vehicle settings, climate, and media.
    *   **Passenger Display:** Allows the front passenger to watch OTT content (YouTube, Netflix), play games, or make video calls privately.
*   **Qualcomm Snapdragon 8295 Chipset:** High-performance hardware with 24GB RAM and 128GB Storage to ensure the screens and OS run smoothly.

### 2. Smart Interior Gadgets
*   **Auto Seat Slide (Welcome Feature):** The driver’s seat automatically slides back when the door is opened for easier entry/exit, then returns to the preset position.
*   **EyeDentity Monitoring System (DOMS):** A roof-mounted AI camera that monitors driver fatigue/distraction, enables in-car selfies with filters, and supports video calls (Zoom/Teams) while parked.
*   **Alexa Integration:** Deep voice control that manages vehicle functions like the sunroof, climate, and music.

### 3. Audio & Experience
*   **Harman Kardon Audio:** A 16-speaker, 1400W system featuring **Dolby Atmos** and 14 amplifiers for an immersive "amphitheater" experience.
*   **SonicSuite (Soundscapes):** Customizable artificial engine sounds, ranging from futuristic electric hums to simulated high-performance engine roars.
*   **Ambient Lighting:** 16-million color options that can sync with music and the "Infinity Glass Roof."

### 4. Advanced Driving Tech
*   **Auto Parking:** A one-touch autonomous parking system where the car handles steering, braking, and gear shifts to park itself.
*   **Augmented Reality HUD:** A Heads-Up Display that projects navigation arrows and safety alerts directly onto the windshield, overlaying them on the road.
*   **Level 2+ ADAS:** Includes Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Keep Assist, and Automatic Emergency Braking powered by 5 radars and 1 vision camera.
*   **Regen Paddle Shifters:** Used to adjust regenerative braking intensity rather than changing gears.

### 5. Exterior & Convenience
*   **Flush Door Handles:** Aerodynamic handles that sit flush with the body and deploy electrically as the driver approaches.
*   **Infinity Glass Roof:** A large panoramic roof designed to enhance the cabin's spatial feel.
*   **Fast Charging:** Supports 175kW DC fast charging, allowing a 20% to 80% charge in approximately 20 minutes.
*   **Frunk (Front Trunk):** Additional storage space under the hood.

### 6. Productivity & Connectivity
*   **65W Type-C Charging:** High-power ports capable of charging laptops and tablets directly.
*   **NFC Key:** Support for unlocking and starting the car using a smartphone or an NFC card.
*   **In-Car App Store:** Access to over 60 pre-installed apps for shopping, news, games, and productivity.
