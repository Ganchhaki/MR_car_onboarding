Slide: Visual Ergonomics
Title: Designing for the Eye
Subtitle: Comfort-first UI placement
3 points:

Focal depth 1–2m from driver eyes, panels hover just past the steering column to eliminate vergence-accommodation conflict
World-locked anchors only — no head-following UI, all overlays pinned firmly to physical dashboard surfaces
High-contrast components: glowing cyan on dark translucent backdrops, large sans-serif text calibrated to headset resolution

Slide: Hardware & Session Management
Title: 10 Minutes. Then Rest.
Subtitle: Designed around headset physical limits
3 points:

Session capped at 10–15 minutes — headset weight and thermal heat create discomfort beyond this window
Passthrough latency demands slow, deliberate cabin exploration — audio narrative pacing enforces this naturally
All interaction audio cues and flow designed to discourage fast head movements that cause anchor drift

Slide: Ray-Cast Interaction Design
Title: Point. Don't Reach.
Subtitle: Far-field ray-casting eliminates gorilla arm entirely
4 points:

Oversized hitboxes 20–30% larger than visible button — absorbs tremor, prevents slip-off during pinch
Magnetic snapping: cursor gravity-wells to button center when ray gets close — feels effortless and magical
No solid laser beam — subtle fading dotted line or surface-only glowing cursor preserves cabin aesthetics
Distinct hover states: target glows brighter, scales up 5–10%, emits low-pitch hum before pinch confirmation

Slide: Voice + Ray-Cast Synergy
Title: Point and Speak
Subtitle: Two modalities. Zero friction.
3 points:

Point-and-speak: ray targets an object, voice executes — "Tell me about this" / "Next" with no pinch needed
Voice as universal escape hatch: globally available at all times regardless of head position or line-of-sight
Hands-free fallback removes all pressure from physical gesture execution during relaxed cabin exploration

Slide: In-Motion Safety Lockout
Title: Drive Mode = Voice Only
Subtitle: Physical safety overrides interaction design
3 points:

When car enters Drive: ray-cast pinch system fully disabled, toast notification appears — "Use Voice Commands"
During ADAS demo: no hand must leave the steering wheel — voice and gaze-dwell are the only active inputs
Safety handoff is seamless: the mode switch feels like a natural transition, not a system failure or limitation
