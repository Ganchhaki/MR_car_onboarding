OPERATIONAL LOGIC

10 Minutes. Then Rest.

Designing within the physical limits of current-gen MR hardware — in a showroom, not a moving cabin.

Session Cap: 10–15 min window to avoid thermal discomfort and headset fatigue.
Stationary Advantage: No motion sickness risk.

INTERACTION MODALITY

Point. Don't Reach.

Far-field ray-casting eliminates "gorilla arm" fatigue, allowing users to control the entire Triple Screen array from a relaxed seated position.

Ray-Cast Precision: Laser-accurate targeting.
Magnetic Snapping: Intelligent cursor "gravity."

SPATIAL ANCHORING

Spatial Anchoring

Every overlay is locked to the parked XEV 9e — not the road ahead.

All MR overlays are world-locked to physical XEV 9e surfaces — dashboard, steering wheel, console.
Tracking is fused with car telemetry/IMU data; UI bounces in perfect sync with the physical dashboard.
Result: Zero drift between digital anchor and physical button — stable for the entire stationary session, even with minor chassis flex from the user entering or exiting the seat.

FEASIBILITY

Constraints

Stationary-Only Scope

The entire MR session is strictly restricted to parked vehicles or showroom floors. Because bulky MR headsets restrict user movement, this limitation is necessary to ensure maximum safety and comfort, and to completely eliminate the risk of motion sickness and sensory conflict.

Hardware and Thermal Limits

The experience is targeted at Meta Quest-class MR headsets with passthrough capabilities. Due to the physical limits of current-gen hardware, the session is capped at a 10–15 minute window to prevent thermal discomfort and headset fatigue.
